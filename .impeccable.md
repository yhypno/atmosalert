## Design Context

### Users
Disaster-management authorities and first responders in India, as specified in
the supplied problem statement. They need to inspect three hazards, forecast
windows, affected places, and meteorological explanations. The landing page
introduces the project to evaluators and prospective users.

### Brand Personality
Grounded, observant, composed. Clear information should support decisions under
pressure. Avoid alarmist marketing or claims of validated forecasting accuracy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light field atlas: paper neutrals, charcoal typography, restrained burnt orange,
and real geographical imagery. This is the working default offered to the user;
no explicit visual preference has been supplied yet. Inspired by the Sand Warm
palette. Use Barlow Condensed for editorial display and Manrope for readable UI.
Rejected reflex choices: Inter, Space Grotesk, and IBM Plex Sans.

### Design Principles
- Put geography and forecast timing at the center of the dashboard.
- Keep demo forecasts visibly distinct from live availability.
- Use flat surfaces and thin dividers, not stacks of floating cards.
- Preserve keyboard navigation, touch targets, contrast, and reduced motion.
- Keep the implementation small: one React frontend and the existing API.
